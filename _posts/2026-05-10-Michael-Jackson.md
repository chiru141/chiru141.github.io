Watched the movie "Michael" this week. 
This was the first time I was introduced to his songs and story.

His moonwalk was only thing I knew about, until last week.
While I didn't know most of the details from his life, one thing particularly caught my attention even in the movie - How lonely he probably was. He exhibited authority in his professional side, but he let his innocence reveal in his personal life.

I saw the recreations of Michael Jackson's tours and shows in the movie and on the various youtube videos and reels I watched after the movie. I couldn't fathom the fan following he had. 

That brought me to the question - what made him great - the greatest of all time?
for that matter, what makes anyone so great?

That they are able to move and connect millions of people, and evoke feelings so strong that lead to crying, epiphanies, and describing the experience as seeing God himself? *(Thinking about Mary Kom, Milkha singh - how when she fought and he ran, they prompted people around them to feel their greatness)*

But this week was about Michael,

I have been listening to his songs the whole week, still haven't gone through all of them. Watched a few of his official videos. I particularly and definitely like the songs *I just can't stop loving you*, *Man in the mirror*, *Beat it*. I can see myself liking more and more.

He uses high-pitch for most of his songs, I didn't know people liked that; a challenging feat to have that pitch apparently. 

I started listening to his songs from his Jackson 5 days. I could feel the change in his voice as he grew up in front of his audience from age 5 to 50.

I watched a few interviews where hosts have asked him rude questions, insinuating crimes he pleaded he didn't commit. They go on to question him about his whacky face and choices. 

If people thought he was so great and millions expressed their love for him, why did they give him such a hard time? why were they so rude to him? why did he have so many allegations against him? was it intrigue about his looks and greatness, or was it jealousy? or was there something even bigger? 

I heard his speeches on various award shows over the years. It made me feel like he kept crying for help and people couldn't hear him and he knew that. His giggle was suggestive of his wisdom and a reflective of his humility. He didn't stop trying.

While I cannot put a definitive finger of what led him to great music, *(ofcourse there are technical reasons for it, but those are results; not the reason)*, he was great because he knew music was only a medium. He cared about things far beyond himself and his music.

While I will still stay on my quest to find the answers to my *greatness* question, If I ever get a chance to meet Michael Jackson, I would like to ask him: *if he could choose, would he have chosen to live an ordinary and a more happier life? Would he have traded his greatness and success for a loving father, a childhood filled with toys, books and playgrounds?*

To honour the legend, and give wind to my inspiration, I would like to end with the following lines

**If the world is seeing you shine outside,** 
**It's cause you are burning inside**
**Will you ever know if it was worth it?**
**Probably not, especially not until you have done it** *(you need to add the music yourself :P)*
